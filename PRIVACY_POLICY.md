# Privacy Policy for Ruby Isleways

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

Welcome to **Ruby Isleways** ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our players, especially children and families. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information through our mobile application.

---

### 1. Compliance with Children's Privacy Laws (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Ruby Isleways is designed as a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for children of all ages. We strictly adhere to:
* The **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** in the United States.
* The **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** and **GDPR-K** in the European Union.
* **Google Play Families Policy** requirements.

We **do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII)** from children or any users.

---

### 2. Information We DO NOT Collect

To maintain a safe, zero-friction environment for children:
* **No Account Creation / No Personal Data:** We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, ages, birthdays, or passwords.
* **No Device Sensors or Permissions:** The app does not request access to your device’s microphone, camera, contacts, photo library, or precise GPS location.
* **No User-to-User Communication:** There is no in-game chat, messaging, or social networking capability.

---

### 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide game functionality, the app stores game state locally on your device using local storage (`SharedPreferences`):
* Puzzle level progression and unlocked islands.
* Star tallies and level completion records.
* Audio preferences (music and sound effects volume/mute toggles).

**This data remains strictly on your device** and is never uploaded to any external or remote server.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

Ruby Isleways is a free-to-play application supported by advertising through **Google AdMob**, an officially certified Google Play Families Ad Network.

To comply with children's privacy standards:
* **Child-Directed Treatment:** All advertising requests are tagged with `TagForChildDirectedTreatment` (COPPA compliant) and `MaxAdContentRating: G`.
* **Non-Personalized Ads Only:** AdMob is restricted to serving contextual, non-personalized, family-appropriate advertisements. Advertisers cannot track users or build interest profiles.
* **Advertising Identifier (GAID):** Google Mobile Ads SDK may access the Google Advertising ID solely for essential non-tracking operations such as frequency capping (limiting how often an ad appears), aggregate reporting, and fraud/security detection.

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, review Google's Privacy Policy:  
[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

### 5. In-App Purchases (If Applicable)

If you make an optional in-app purchase (e.g., to remove ads), the transaction is processed directly and securely by **Google Play Billing**. We do not collect, view, or store any credit card numbers or financial account information.

---

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store personal data on remote servers, we maintain no database of user records. You can delete all locally stored game progress at any time by clearing the app data in your device settings or uninstalling the application.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the game after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
